Context & Cringe started as a training exercise. We kept noticing the same thing: people don't really *get* privacy risks until they feel the discomfort themselves.

A data point can seem neutral. A feature can seem reasonable. But combine them in the right app context, and suddenly everyone in the room goes quiet.

That moment — the cringe — is the game.

We're launching the site alongside the game to share more of the thinking behind it: how privacy intersects with design, what makes certain combinations feel worse than others, and why instinct is data.
